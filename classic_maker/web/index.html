classic maker

Simple editing. Just like you remember.

I'm tired of using “open source” bloatware. For non-hardcore creators like me, I miss Windows Movie Maker: simple, easy to install, lightweight, and it just works.

Classic Maker keeps that spirit alive. Arrange video, add music or voice, and put a few simple finishing touches on your movie. No animated effects or transitions. No accounts. No cloud.
